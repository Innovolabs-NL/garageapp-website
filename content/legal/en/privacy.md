## 2. Roles: website vs SaaS

- **Website & commercial relationship:** Innovolabs B.V. is the **controller** for contact requests, marketing site preferences, and SaaS account/subscription data we need to provide Motivox to your garage.
- **Shop customer data in Motivox:** When a garage (tenant) stores personal data of its own customers, vehicles, jobs, and invoices in Motivox, that garage is typically the **controller**. Innovolabs B.V. acts as **processor** for that tenant data, under a separate DPA on request. Each shop sees only its own data (tenant isolation).

## 3. Categories of data we process

Depending on how you use Motivox and this website, we may process:

- **Contact form / email:** name, email address, company name, message content
- **Account & subscription:** registration details, roles (office / technician), login identifiers, trial and billing status, Stripe customer and subscription identifiers
- **Support:** tickets and related correspondence
- **Product usage (SaaS):** operational logs needed to run and secure the service (e.g. activity log entries your shop generates)
- **Tenant business content (as processor):** customer and vehicle records, inspections, quotes, work orders, hours, parts, invoices, portal access tokens, optional payment and accounting connection settings your shop configures
- **Website preferences:** theme preference stored in the browser (`localStorage`) — not used for advertising profiles

We do **not** sell personal data.

We use service providers that process data on our instructions, for example:

- **Email delivery** for the marketing-site contact form (e.g. Resend when configured)

Some providers may process data outside the EEA. Where that happens, we use appropriate safeguards (such as EU Standard Contractual Clauses) as required by the AVG.

## 7. Retention

- **Contact messages:** kept as long as needed to handle your request and for a reasonable follow-up period, then deleted or anonymised unless a longer retention is required (e.g. ongoing conversation or legal claim).
- **SaaS accounts & billing records:** for the duration of the subscription and thereafter as required for tax/bookkeeping and dispute handling.
- **Tenant business data:** retained while the garage account is active, subject to the shop’s own retention choices and our processor instructions; after termination we delete or anonymise per the DPA / product features (including export and anonymise requests in the product), unless law requires longer storage.

## 8. Security

We apply organisational and technical measures appropriate to a multi-tenant SaaS product, including authentication, role separation, encryption in transit, and tenant isolation of data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; please use strong passwords and enable MFA for staff accounts.
