## 2. Roles: website vs SaaS

- **Website & commercial relationship:** Innovolabs B.V. is the **controller** for contact requests, marketing site preferences, and SaaS account, subscription and product-update data we need to provide Motivox to your garage.
- **Shop customer data in Motivox:** When a garage (tenant) stores personal data of its own customers, vehicles, jobs, and invoices in Motivox, that garage is the **controller**. Innovolabs B.V. acts as **processor** for that tenant data under the DPA in our terms. Each shop sees only its own data (tenant isolation).

If you are a customer of a garage that uses Motivox, your garage is your first point of contact for questions about your data (see section 9).

## 3. Categories of data we process

Depending on how you use Motivox and this website, we may process:

- **Contact form / email:** name, email address, company name, message content
- **Account & subscription:** registration details, roles (office / technician), login identifiers, trial and billing status, Stripe customer and subscription identifiers
- **Proof of acceptance:** the version of the terms and privacy policy you accepted, the time, and the IP address from which you accepted
- **Support:** tickets and related correspondence
- **Product updates:** your name, email address, language and role, used to send product and service updates (see section 5)
- **Product usage (SaaS):** operational logs needed to run and secure the service (e.g. activity log entries your shop generates)
- **Tenant business content (as processor):** customer and vehicle records, inspections, quotes, work orders, hours, parts, invoices, portal access tokens and one-time login codes, optional payment and accounting connection settings your shop configures
- **Signature evidence (as processor, for the garage):** when a customer approves a quote in the customer portal, we record the consent, time, IP address, browser details (user agent) and the approved PDF as evidence for the garage
- **Website preferences:** theme preference stored in the browser (`localStorage`) — not used for advertising profiles
- **Website server logs:** technical data such as IP address and request time, processed by Vercel, the hosting provider of this website, to deliver and secure it

We do **not** sell personal data. We do not use profiling or make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you.

## 7. Retention

- **Contact messages:** up to 12 months after your request is handled, unless a longer period is needed (e.g. ongoing conversation or legal claim).
- **SaaS accounts:** for the duration of the subscription. When a garage owner deletes the garage account, a 14-day grace period applies before the data is deleted.
- **Billing records:** 7 years, as required by Dutch tax and bookkeeping rules.
- **Proof of acceptance:** for the duration of the account and 5 years afterwards, for dispute handling.
- **Product update send log:** 365 days.
- **Tenant business data:** retained while the garage account is active, subject to the shop’s own retention setting and our processor instructions. After termination we delete or anonymise it as described in the DPA, unless law requires longer storage. Invoices may be retained by the garage for fiscal reasons even when personal contact data is anonymised.
- **Backups:** deleted data disappears from backups in the normal backup rotation.

## 8. Security

We apply organisational and technical measures appropriate to a multi-tenant SaaS product, including authentication and optional multi-factor authentication, role separation, encryption in transit, tenant isolation of data, malware scanning of uploads, and hosting in the EU. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; please use strong passwords and enable MFA for staff accounts.

## 10. Cookies and local storage

Motivox only uses cookies and browser storage that are needed to run the service or to remember your settings. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not use analytics that follow you across websites. Because of this, we do not show a cookie banner.

**This website** uses **localStorage** only to remember your light/dark theme preference. It is strictly functional.

**The Motivox application** uses the following:

| Name | Type | Purpose | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|
| `GarageApp.Auth` | Cookie, necessary | Keeps staff signed in | Session / until sign-out or expiry |
| `GarageApp.Customer` | Cookie, necessary | Keeps customers signed in to the customer portal | Session / until sign-out or expiry |
| `GarageApp.AntiXsrf` | Cookie, necessary | Protects forms against cross-site request forgery | Session |
| `gm_mode` | Cookie, functional | Remembers your chosen work mode | 1 year |
| `.AspNetCore.Culture` | Cookie, functional | Remembers your language | 1 year |
| `garageapp.themePreference` | Local storage, functional | Remembers light/dark theme | Until you clear it |
| Menu and tour state | Local storage, functional | Remembers whether the side menu is open and whether you finished the first-run tour | Until you clear it |

**Payments.** When you pay or manage a subscription, our payment provider Stripe loads its script on our pages and may set cookies to prevent fraud and process payments securely. These are necessary for the payment to work. See Stripe's privacy policy for details.

**Fonts.** Fonts are served from our own servers. No data is sent to font providers.

You can delete or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings. If you block the necessary cookies, you will not be able to sign in.
