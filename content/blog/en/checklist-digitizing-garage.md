Digitizing isn’t “install a package.” It’s choosing processes, agreeing roles, and getting adoption on the floor.

## Checklist

1. Document the job flow from intake to invoice
2. Define roles (inspection, planning, invoicing)
3. Build checklist templates you reuse weekly
4. Decide what customers may see in a portal
5. Plan data import (customers, vehicles, open jobs)
6. Pilot one location with a short feedback loop
7. Train techs on time first; back office on invoicing next
8. Measure status calls, invoice errors, time-to-invoice

Online booking from the homepage and payment gateways without setup can wait. The core win is inspection → quote → approval → hours → invoice. An appointment slot *after* approval belongs in that chain.
