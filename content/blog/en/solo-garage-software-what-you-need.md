If you work alone or with one helper, you don't want a package built for a twenty-person shop. But a notebook or an Excel file stops working once customers expect a clean quote, status updates, and an invoice. **Garage software for solo mechanics** should start small and still cover the whole job.

## What a solo garage really needs

1. **Licence plate lookup.** Pulling vehicle details and APK date from RDW saves typing and mistakes.
2. **Inspection and quote.** Findings become lines on a quote the customer can approve digitally, with signature and timestamp.
3. **Work order with hours.** A timer or manual hours, plus parts and consumables, in one place.
4. **A proper VAT invoice.** A PDF with Dutch VAT, plus credit notes when something needs correcting.
5. **One account for office and workshop.** Switch between desk and shop floor without a second subscription.
6. **Accounting integration.** Moneybird, Exact Online, e-Boekhouden, or an export as CSV or UBL.

## What you don't need yet

Live shop planning for several technicians, detailed team reporting, and dozens of accounts. Those belong to a growing team and can be added later.
