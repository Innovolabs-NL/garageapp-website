Every “is my car ready?” call costs time. A good portal replaces part of that with transparent status: job progress, relevant inspection/plan info, and invoices (summary + PDF).

Secure OTP or magic-link access, plus time-limited token links for specific jobs, keeps the barrier low. GarageApp ships a customer portal beside back-office and tech portals — one data source.
