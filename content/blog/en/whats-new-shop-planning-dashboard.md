A garage does not fail on the big things. It fails on Monday at 08:05, when three cars are due, one was dropped off in the night, and nobody is sure which bay is free. This round of Motivox updates is about that moment.

## Live shop planning (Garage plan)

Arrivals, night-drop cars, replanned appointments, and real shop capacity now live on one board. A team cockpit shows who is clocked on and what they have on. Leave requests are handled by the team themselves instead of by a sticky note on the office door.

Live shop planning is part of the **Garage** plan, and because the 30-day trial is the full Garage version, you can try it on your own cars before you choose.

## Solo mode for ZZP

Working alone, or with one helper? On ZZP one account now switches between the office and the workshop, and you are planned as a technician yourself. You count once towards your plan: ZZP includes you plus one extra account (office or technician).

## A dashboard that is yours

Choose which tiles you see, drag them into the order you look at them, and make them bigger or smaller. New tiles:

- **Low stock**, set a minimum level per product and the tile tells you what to reorder
- **Technician workload**, who has room today, and who does not

## Onboarding that asks the right question first

Setup now starts by asking whether you work solo or with a team, then walks you through what your shop actually needs. A Getting Started card stays on the dashboard until you are through it.

## The boring-but-valuable list

- Line discounts on labour and materials
- Credit notes
- Kilometres or miles per vehicle
- Customers sign in with a one-time code, and one login covers more than one Motivox garage
