## Where others are stronger or cheaper

- **Price.** EsGarage (€15.95 per month) and GarageManager (from €39) cost less than Motivox. If you only need invoices, work orders, and customer records, that is a serious argument.
- **Offline.** EsGarage runs locally and needs no internet connection. Motivox runs in the browser and needs internet.
- **Accounting integrations.** GarageManager lists more integrations (including SnelStart, Rompslomp, Yuki, and Jortt) than Motivox.
- **Mobile app.** GarageOS lists an iOS/Android app. Motivox is a web application with no separate app.

## Where Motivox is a good fit

- **One file for office, technician, and customer.** Three portals on the same data: the office plans and invoices, the technician logs hours with a timer, and the customer follows status and approves work with digital signature and timestamp.
- **Live shop planning.** Arrivals, cars dropped off after hours, replanning appointments, and capacity, included in the Garage plan.
- **No retyping.** From inspection to quote, work order, hours, and VAT invoice without re-entering the same data.
- **Clear pricing.** Two plans, cancel monthly, a 30-day trial with everything in Garage.

## How to choose

1. **Working alone, small budget, no portal needed?** Look at EsGarage or GarageManager Starter.
2. **Customer portal and digital job cards important?** Compare GarageOS and Motivox.
3. **A team that needs planning and several technicians?** Compare the planning in GarageOS, GarageManager Professional, and Motivox Garage.
4. **A specific accounting package?** Check first that yours (such as SnelStart or Yuki) is supported.

Most vendors in this list offer a trial or monthly cancellation, apart from SimpelGarage's rental option. Try two with your own work orders.
